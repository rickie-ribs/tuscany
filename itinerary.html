Day 1
Arrival & Settling into Radda
July 4 · Saturday
Morning
Florence arrival + drive to Radda
Fly into Florence, collect rental cars (two cars recommended for 6), and drive south through the Chianti hills to Radda (~1h 10m). The drive itself is part of the experience — the SS222 Chiantigiana through Greve is one of the great scenic roads in Tuscany.
SS222 via Greve in Chianti — windows down
~9am depart FLR · arrive Radda ~11am
Afternoon / Early Evening
Check in · Explore Radda on foot
Check in, have lunch at La Bottega di Giovannino (Via Roma 6 — open since 1965, excellent cured meats and Chianti Classico by the glass). Walk Radda's medieval walls and the Enoteca Bottega del Chianti Classico — the official Consortium shop — for an informal intro to the region's producers.
Town exploring
Lunch ~€25–35/person · Enoteca tasting free
~11am–1pm check-in & lunch · 2–4pm town walk · Pool 4–6pm
Evening
Dinner at Relais Vignale
First night — keep it easy. Dine at the hotel's Ristorante Vignale. The terrace views over the vineyards at dusk are the ideal arrival moment. Classic Tuscan set menu; pair with a glass from the estate wine shop as a warm-up to the days ahead.
Hotel dinner · confirmed
~€45–65/person incl. wine
~7pm drinks on terrace · 8:00pm dinner — confirmed
No scheduled wine activity today — let the drive through Chianti and the enoteca visit serve as a gentle introduction. Save the energy for Day 2.
Day 2
Castellina in Chianti & Castelvecchi Winery
July 5 · Sunday
Morning
Castellina in Chianti — Via delle Volte
Drive 12 min west to Castellina in Chianti. Walk Via delle Volte — the ancient covered tunnel running inside the medieval walls with openings looking out over the valley. Browse the small enotecas and ceramics shops; pick up bottles to bring home. Stop at Rampini Ceramics nearby — some of the finest hand-painted Tuscan ceramics in the region.
Medieval town
~12 min · Free entry · Budget for bottles + ceramics
~9:30am depart · 10am–12pm Castellina · back by 12:15pm
Afternoon
Castelvecchi Winery — "From the Barrel to the Bottle"
Hotel-arranged private tour of Castelvecchi Winery — a guided visit covering the full production process from barrel aging to bottling, paired with a board of local Chianti products. Transfer included via hotel. Return to Relais Vignale by approximately 4:00pm.
Winery tour · confirmed
Confirmed: Hotel departure 12:30pm · return ~4:00pm · arranged through Relais Vignale. Afternoon pool afterwards.
Evening
Dinner — Osteria Volpaia or in Radda
Two easy options after the winery tour: drive 7 min up to Volpaia for dinner at Osteria Volpaia (excellent, farm-driven, right in the village piazza), or stay in Radda and dine at one of the town's restaurants — La Bottega di Giovannino or Ristorante Il Vignale are both solid choices a short walk from the hotel.
Town dinner
Osteria Volpaia ~€40–55/person · Radda restaurants ~€30–45/person
~7:30pm dinner (Volpaia 7 min drive · or walking distance in Radda)
Day 3
Siena & Tenuta di Carleone
July 6 · Monday
Morning
Siena — Private Tour with Roberta Marioni
Guided private tour of Siena's historic centre with Roberta Marioni — the Piazza del Campo, the Duomo di Siena (one of Italy's most spectacular Gothic churches), and the Piccolomini Library. Meet at 10:00am. Contact: +39 347 338 7795 · guidasiena.it
Private tour · confirmed
~9:20am depart · 10:00am tour with Roberta — confirmed · ~12:30pm lunch in Siena
Afternoon
Tenuta di Carleone — Classic Tasting
Walk 10 min from the hotel to Tenuta di Carleone — a boutique biodynamic estate in the Radda hills producing some of the most sought-after Chianti Classico in the appellation. Classic Tasting for 6, reserved at 3pm. Small production, intensely personal, and a very different experience from the larger estate tours. No need for a car — walk straight from Relais Vignale.
Classic Tasting · confirmed 3pm
10 min walk from Relais Vignale
~1pm lunch in Siena · return to Radda ~2:30pm · 3:00pm Carleone tasting — confirmed, 6 guests

After a full day in Siena and the Carleone tasting, an easy evening. Dine at Ristorante Vignale on the hotel terrace, or walk into Radda for dinner at one of the town's restaurants. Pre-dinner drinks on the rooftop terrace as the light fades over the Chianti hills is hard to beat.

Alternative & Optional Activities

Swap into any day, or add to a free afternoon

Truffle Hunting Outdoors
A 2h private hunt with a local trufarolo and their dogs through the oak woods near Radda. July yields summer truffles — lighter than winter black, but the hunting experience is the point. Ends with a tasting paired with local oils and wine. Groups of 6 are ideal. ~€60–80/person.
Best swap: replace the Castellina morning on Day 2, or add to any free afternoon.
Chianti Cooking Class Hands-on
A 3h private class for 6 covering fresh pasta, ribollita, and a Tuscan dessert. Two good options: Vignale Cooking Classes on-site or Badia a Coltibuono's Cooking School (15 min away, consistently top-rated). Typically runs as a morning session ending with eating what you made. ~€80–120/person.
Best swap: morning of Day 2 or Day 4 in place of the Castellina visit or hike.
E-Bike Tour through the Vineyards Active
Guided e-bike tours out of Radda — typically 3–4h covering 20–30km of Chianti Classico backroads through vineyards, olive groves, and hilltop villages. Chianti by Bike and DiVine Tours (Radda-based) both well-reviewed. Good for a morning where you want to earn the afternoon tasting. ~€50–70/person.
Best fit: morning of Day 3 instead of Siena, or a standalone active day.
Castello di Albola or Monterinaldi Winery
Two strong fourth-winery options. Castello di Albola (Radda, ~10 min) is a classic Chianti Classico producer with castle views and a well-regarded tasting room. Castello Monterinaldi (also Radda zone) is a smaller, intimate family estate with a sommelier-led cellar tour and food pairing. ~€25–45/person.
Best slot: a free afternoon, or swap in for the Castellina morning on Day 2.
Hot Air Balloon over Chianti Scenic
Dawn departure (5:30–6am), 1–1.5h flight, champagne breakfast on landing. Bookable through the Vignale concierge. July weather generally favorable but subject to wind. ~€250–320/person.
Early morning on any day — pairs perfectly with a relaxed afternoon at the hotel pool.
